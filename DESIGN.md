# Design

## Existing direction

The interface uses a dark charcoal/green palette, restrained bright-green accents, Space Grotesk display text, and Manrope body text. Its signal, waveform, and orbital motifs connect voice, understanding, and action. The landing page is editorial and spacious; the dashboard uses focused operational sections. Styling is implemented in plain CSS.

## Design principles

- Make the product feel premium, modern, trustworthy, polished, and business-focused.
- Keep its visual identity distinct from generic AI dashboards.
- Preserve the existing palette, typography, spacing, and visual language when adding functionality. Do not redesign the whole project for an individual feature.
- Use motion selectively and respect reduced-motion preferences.
- Keep layouts clear and purposeful; avoid excessive cards, gradients, glass effects, and decoration.
- Avoid generic purple AI styling, fake logos, and unnecessary animations.
- Keep demo content recognizable as demo content. Do not present invented statistics or sample activity as live business results.
